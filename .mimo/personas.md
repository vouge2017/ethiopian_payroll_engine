# EthioPayroll — User Personas

Design for these people. Every UI decision gets tested against them.

---

## TIGIST (Primary — design for her first)

- **Role:** Business owner
- **Age:** 45
- **Tech:** Phone only, no laptop
- **Accounting:** None — never understood tax brackets
- **Language:** Amharic only
- **Needs:** Simple, fast, trustworthy
- **Fears:** Making mistakes, getting penalized by ERCA
- **Goal:** Run payroll in under 10 minutes on her phone
- **Test question:** Can Tigist do this without calling her accountant?

---

## DAWIT

- **Role:** HR officer
- **Age:** 28
- **Tech:** Phone and laptop
- **Accounting:** Some knowledge
- **Language:** English and Amharic
- **Needs:** Efficient, accurate, compliant
- **Fears:** Missing deadlines, wrong calculations
- **Goal:** Process payroll and file ERCA on time
- **Test question:** Does this save Dawit time vs Excel?

---

## HANA

- **Role:** Employee (factory worker)
- **Age:** 22
- **Tech:** Phone only, no email
- **Accounting:** None
- **Language:** Amharic
- **Needs:** Understand her payslip, check leave balance
- **Fears:** Not understanding why deductions were made
- **Goal:** See her net pay and understand each deduction
- **Test question:** Can Hana read her payslip without asking anyone?

---

## ABEBE

- **Role:** Accountant (external)
- **Age:** 35
- **Tech:** Laptop primarily
- **Accounting:** Professional — manages 15 companies
- **Language:** English
- **Needs:** Multi-client view, bulk operations, reports
- **Fears:** Client getting penalized for late filing
- **Goal:** Switch between companies and file reports fast
- **Test question:** Can Abebe manage 15 companies without confusion?

---

## How to Use These Personas

When building any feature, ask:

1. **Would Tigist understand this?** If not, simplify.
2. **Would Hana be confused by this payslip?** If yes, add explanations.
3. **Would Dawit use this vs his current Excel sheet?** If not, rethink value.
4. **Would Abebe need this for his 15 clients?** If not, it's probably not MVP.

When the personas conflict, **Tigist wins**. She's the buyer.
