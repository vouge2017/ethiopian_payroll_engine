# Skill: Ethiopian User Experience

You are the frontend developer building for
Ethiopian SME owners and employees.

---

## User Personas

### TIGIST (Primary — design for her first)
- Business owner, 45, no accounting background
- Uses phone for everything
- Needs: simple, fast, Amharic, trustworthy
- Fears: making mistakes, getting penalized
- Goal: payroll in under 10 minutes

### DAWIT
- HR officer, 28, some accounting knowledge
- Uses phone and laptop
- Needs: efficient, accurate, compliant
- Fears: missing deadlines, wrong calculations

### HANA
- Employee, 22, factory worker
- Phone only, no email
- Needs: understand payslip, check/request leave
- Fears: not understanding deductions

### ABEBE
- Accountant, 35, manages 15 companies
- Needs: multi-client view, bulk operations
- Fears: client getting penalized

---

## Design Rules

### Mobile First
- Design for 360px width
- Touch targets min 44px
- No hover-dependent interactions
- Works on slow 3G connections

### Amharic First
- All UI in Amharic by default
- English as switch option
- Native speaker review required
- Error messages in helpful Amharic

### No Email Required
- Login: phone + OTP
- Notifications: SMS or in-app
- Never require email for any flow

### Speed Targets
- Sign up to first payroll: under 1 hour
- Add one employee: under 2 minutes
- Run payroll (20 people): under 5 minutes
- View payslip: under 10 seconds

### Ethiopian Calendar
- Date picker shows Ethiopian dates
- Dual display: "01 ጥቅምት 2018 (11 Oct 2025)"
- Pay periods use Ethiopian months

### Explainability
- Every number on payslip is tappable
- Tapping shows calculation breakdown
- Language: simple enough for factory worker

### Cultural Fit
- Salary privacy (no accidental exposure)
- Green = success
- Professional, respectful tone
- Sealed payslip delivery (each employee sees only own)
