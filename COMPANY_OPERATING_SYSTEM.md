## Week 1: Establish Governance (No Coding, No Features)

### Board 1: Product Steering Committee

**Purpose:** Owns the roadmap.
**Meeting:** Every Monday, 60 minutes
**Members:**
- Product Director (Chair)
- Engineering Lead
- UX Lead
- Customer Success
- Compliance Lead

**Outputs:**
- Sprint Goal
- Approved Features
- Delayed Features
- Risks
- Decisions

---

### Board 2: Compliance Board

**Purpose:** Protect legal correctness.
**Meeting:** Every two weeks
**Members:**
- Senior Accountant
- Auditor
- Payroll Specialist
- Labor Law Expert

**Outputs:**
- Compliance Report: Approved / Rejected / Needs Clarification

---

### Board 3: Customer Advisory Board

**Purpose:** Protect usability.
**Meeting:** Every month
**Members:** 10 customers, different industries

**Agenda:**
- What frustrated you?
- What saved time?
- What still requires Excel?
- What did you never use?
- Would you pay?
- How much?
- Would you recommend us?

---

### Board 4: Implementation Board

**Purpose:** Protect adoption.
**Meeting:** Every two weeks
**Members:**
- Implementation Team
- Support
- Training
- Sales

**Agenda:**
- Where do customers fail?
- Where do they need help?
- What takes longest?
- Which questions repeat?
- Can onboarding become easier?

## Product Decision Framework

Every feature must pass all five gates. One "No" kills the feature.

| Gate | Question | Owner |
|------|----------|-------|
| Customer Gate | Did a customer actually ask for this? | Customer Board |
| Business Gate | Does it save money or time? | Product Committee |
| Compliance Gate | Is it legally correct? | Compliance Board |
| Technical Gate | Can we maintain it? | Engineering |
| Adoption Gate | Will SMEs actually use it? | Implementation Board |

## The Kill List (Monthly Review)

Every month, review every feature. For each:

- Is it used?
- Does it solve a problem?
- Does it create complexity?
- Can it be simplified?
- Can we automate it?
- Should we remove it?

**The best product teams ask: "What can we remove while making the product more valuable?"**

## Payroll Excellence Office (PEO)

**Permanent cross-functional group reporting directly to Product Director.**

**Mission:** Build the most trusted payroll platform in Ethiopia.

### Members

| Function | Role |
|----------|------|
| **Product** | Product Director (Chair), Product Manager |
| **Engineering** | Engineering Lead, QA Lead |
| **Compliance** | Certified Accountant, Auditor, Labor Law Advisor |
| **Customer** | HR Representative, Payroll Officer, SME Business Owner |
| **Operations** | Customer Success Lead, Implementation Lead |

### Responsibilities

- Approve the roadmap
- Validate compliance before release
- Review pilot feedback
- Decide priorities based on customer evidence
- Monitor business KPIs
- Approve production releases

### Golden Rule

No major feature reaches production unless all five perspectives agree:

- **Customer:** "This solves a real problem."
- **Compliance:** "This is legally correct."
- **Engineering:** "This is reliable and maintainable."
- **Operations:** "Customers can adopt and support it."
- **Product:** "This aligns with our long-term vision."
