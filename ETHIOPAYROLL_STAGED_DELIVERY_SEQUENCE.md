# EthioPayroll — Staged Delivery Sequence

**Status:** Proposed sequence for approval — not an implementation authorization
**Prepared:** 2 November 2026

The North Star is long-term. The sequence below moves only through evidence gates. It does not declare any gate complete based on old documents, isolated tests or a demo.

| Stage | Entry criteria | Activities | Exit evidence | Stop conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Product decisions and evidence | Management reviews the North Star/boundary; current task and repository baseline are known. | Resolve first customer to validate, business/accountant responsibility, first-phase scope, calculation authority, payment/submission responsibility and privacy prerequisites. Interview target users; obtain professional/provider evidence. | Recorded decisions; current source/legal register; owner and evidence gap for each unresolved item; baseline approval record. | No requirements approval if buyer/problem, responsibility or scope remains contradictory. External evidence cannot be invented by management. |
| 1. Internal synthetic end-to-end validation | Approved bounded requirements; isolated synthetic tenant/test environment; no real employee data or external payment/submission. | Trace company/employee, salary history, payroll inputs, calculation, explanations, exceptions, review, approval, locked outputs, reports, exports and correction/reversal. Reconcile all outputs. | Reproducible employee-level expected results tied to approved rule sources/interpretations; cross-company isolation/role tests; locked history, recovery and output reconciliations; exact-SHA test receipt. | Any unexplained calculation discrepancy, tenant exposure, unauthorized approval, history mutation, data loss or unrepeatable output. |
| 2. One supervised real-business parallel payroll | Stage 1 exit; willing business and qualified accountant; written scope/consent; privacy/hosting review; case calculations professionally reviewed; payment/filing responsibilities agreed. | Run the period in EthioPayroll and the customer’s existing authoritative process; customer keeps control of real filing/payment. Compare gross/net, tax, pension, allowances, deductions, payslips, payment instructions, tax/pension schedules, exports, corrections and audit trail. | Written accountant review: accepted or listed objections; every discrepancy has cause, correction, retest and disposition; no unauthorized external action. | Any unresolved material calculation/legal issue, cross-company access, wrong approver, corrupted history, unsafe payment instruction or missing evidence. |
| 3. Three-business controlled pilot | Stage 2 discrepancies closed or explicitly judged non-blocking by accountable management/professional; each business consents and has a qualified reviewer. | Repeat controlled cycles across three independent businesses with different but bounded setups; measure time, manual steps, errors, support effort, output reconciliation and recovery. | Written acceptance/objections per business; repeatable workflow; legal and technical controls; support/cost evidence; no critical open issue. | Any critical issue recurs, only one accountant can operate it with developer intervention, or results require Excel as calculation authority. |
| 4. Commercial-release decision | Stage 3 exit; legal/official sources current; privacy/security and operations ready; pricing/support evidence; release owner assigned. | Management reviews product, operational, commercial and legal readiness separately; approves exact release scope, limits, support, incident response, backups and rollback. | Recorded go/no-go tied to exact release SHA and environment; known limitations and customer terms; successful recovery/support drill. | No release with unapproved statutory rules relied upon, unresolved high-impact security/privacy risk, missing support/restore or misleading payment/filing states. |
| 5. Ten-business adoption milestone | Commercial-release evidence and support capacity; selected customers fit proven envelope. | Add businesses gradually; observe tenant isolation, onboarding, portfolio needs, concurrent month-close workloads, provider failures and cost-to-serve. | Ten-business evidence pack: cycles completed, reconciliation, issue rates, support load, performance and customer value/retention evidence. | Pause after any critical access/calculation/history/recovery incident or if support capacity is exceeded. |
| 6. Later scale and automation | Ten-business gate passes; repeat demand and provider access/contracts documented; architecture/security review approved. | Prioritize measured friction: attendance connectors, employee self-service, bank/payment orchestration, tax/pension submission, accounting connectors, multilingual conversational assistance or AI. | Per-feature legal/provider/technical/customer acceptance; explicit safe states, recovery and monitoring. | No feature advances solely because a competitor offers it or the North Star mentions it. |

## Sequence rules

- The first supervised run is a **parallel shadow**: the business’s existing approved payroll remains authoritative until it explicitly accepts a result and independently controls payment/filing.
- A successful synthetic run proves only that scenario in that environment; it is not legal approval, provider acceptance or customer use.
- The three-business pilot is not commercial release. The ten-business milestone is later adoption evidence, not a permanent employee-size limit.
- For any financial provider, distinguish prepared, authorized, initiated, settled, rejected/failed and reconciled states. Only provider evidence establishes settlement.
- For statutory reporting, distinguish prepared, submitted, acknowledged, accepted/rejected, paid and reconciled.
- Future attendance, voice, AI, integrations and automation are selected by measured need and feasibility, not by this sequence alone.
