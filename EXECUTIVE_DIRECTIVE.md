# Executive Product Directive
### Ethiopian Payroll & Workforce Platform
**Version 1.0 | From: Product Director | Effective: 2026-07-28**

---

## Executive Decision

After reviewing the Product & Market Readiness Report, the leadership team acknowledges the exceptional engineering work completed.

The platform demonstrates a mature payroll engine, strong security architecture, configurable compliance rules, and a solid technical foundation.

**However, engineering readiness does not equal market readiness.**

The next phase of this project is not software development.
The next phase is market validation, compliance verification, and customer adoption.

Therefore, effective immediately, the project's primary objective changes.

**We are no longer optimizing for features.**
**We are optimizing for trust.**

---

## Strategic Objective

The mission of this platform is not to calculate payroll.

Its mission is:

> **To become the most trusted payroll and workforce platform for Ethiopian SMEs.**

Every future decision must increase one or more of the following:

- Customer trust
- Compliance confidence
- Ease of payroll processing
- Time saved
- Business insight

If a feature does not contribute to these outcomes, it should not be prioritized.

---

## Executive Findings

| Area | Assessment |
|------|-----------|
| Engineering | **Strong.** Payroll engine sufficiently mature for controlled pilots. |
| Product | **Good.** Direction is clear. |
| Market Validation | **Weak.** No evidence that the product solves highest-priority problems. |
| Compliance | **Incomplete.** Rules exist but not independently verified. |
| Commercial Readiness | **Not Ready.** Pricing, support, onboarding all undefined. |

---

## Executive Decisions

**Decision 1:** Feature expansion is no longer the highest priority. Only features required for pilot customers, compliance, or critical usability issues may be developed.

**Decision 2:** Customer validation becomes the highest priority. Engineering assumptions must be replaced with customer evidence.

**Decision 3:** Every payroll calculation must be independently verified by Ethiopian accounting professionals. Internal confidence is not sufficient.

**Decision 4:** The first release will be a controlled pilot, not a public launch. Success measured by customer outcomes, not software completion.

## Immediate Workstreams

### Workstream A — Compliance Verification
**Owner:** Compliance Lead
**Objective:** Prove that every statutory calculation is correct.
**Deliverables:** Accountant verification, auditor verification, legal verification, ERCA verification, bank file verification, payroll scenario verification.
**Success Criteria:** Zero unresolved compliance issues.

### Workstream B — Customer Discovery
**Owner:** Product Team
**Objective:** Understand how Ethiopian businesses actually process payroll.
**Deliverables:** 50 structured interviews, workflow documentation, pain point analysis, feature validation, industry comparison.
**Output:** Product Discovery Report.

### Workstream C — Pilot Program
**Owner:** Implementation Team
**Objective:** Run real payroll. Not demonstrations.
**Deliverables:** Five pilot companies, minimum three payroll cycles each.
**Collect:** Every question, every complaint, every support request, every workaround, every failure.
**Output:** Pilot Findings Report.

### Workstream D — Product Simplification
**Owner:** UX Team
**Objective:** Reduce complexity.
**Measure:** Time to onboard, time to create employee, time to process payroll, time to approve payroll, time to generate reports.
**Target:** Every unnecessary click removed.

### Workstream E — Commercial Readiness
**Owner:** Business Team
**Deliverables:** Pricing strategy, support model, implementation methodology, training material, knowledge base, customer onboarding plan, sales presentation, ROI calculator.

## Product Principles

Every feature proposed must answer:

1. Which customer requested this?
2. Which problem does it solve?
3. How frequently does the problem occur?
4. How much time does it save?
5. How will success be measured?
6. Why should this be built before anything else?

**Features without evidence will not be approved.**

## The 50% Challenge

> "If we had to launch in 60 days, what would we keep?"

This is a **thinking exercise**, not a development task. We are not deleting code. We are identifying what is **mission critical**.

### Layer 1 — Core Product (Must Never Fail)
- Employee management
- Payroll calculation (tax, pension, net)
- Leave management (directly affects payroll)
- Overtime (essential for manufacturing, construction, hospitality)
- ERCA report generation
- Bank file generation
- Payroll approval workflow
- Audit trail

### Layer 2 — Valuable Features (Freeze until Layer 1 is trusted)
- Attendance, accounting export, reports, analytics, employee portal, loans, severance

### Layer 3 — Scale Features (Freeze completely for now)
- API, AI, MFA, Google OAuth, scheduled reports, PWA, multi-company

**Nothing is deleted. Nothing is wasted. We simply stop expanding while we prove the foundation.**

**Key insight:** MVP depends on target customer. Schools/retail → overtime can wait. Factories/hospitals → overtime is core.
