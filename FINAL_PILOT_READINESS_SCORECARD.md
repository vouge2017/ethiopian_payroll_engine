## Recommended pilot scope (immediate next step)

**Stage 1: 1 company × 1 month**

- **Company**: One real Ethiopian business that uses Excel for payroll today
- **Duration**: 1 full monthly cycle
- **Accountant**: A real Ethiopian accountant (NOT the developer)
- **Parallel verification**: Accountant processes the same payroll in Excel and the system; results compared line-by-line
- **Discrepancy log**: Every difference recorded in `docs/pilot_discrepancies.md` with classification
- **Success criteria**: Zero UNRESOLVED critical discrepancies; accountant can complete the workflow without Excel; accountant states they would use the system again
