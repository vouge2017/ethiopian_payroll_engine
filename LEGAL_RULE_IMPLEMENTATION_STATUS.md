## Meaning of statuses

- **APPROVED:** the particular legal provision and implementation rule are supported by an identified source. This does not mean the entire area is legally resolved.
- **CONFIGURABLE PENDING CONFIRMATION:** the product may represent a choice but must label it unconfirmed and must not present it as the legally correct default.
- **BLOCKED:** do not approve or use the disputed production calculation/workflow until the missing source or written interpretation is obtained.
