## Status meanings

- **CONFIRMED LEGAL PROVISION:** The particular provision is supported by an identified legal source; it does not settle every application.
- **PROFESSIONAL INTERPRETATION REQUIRED:** Source text exists but application remains unclear.
- **OFFICIAL GUIDANCE UNVERIFIED:** No identifiable current official guidance establishing the operational claim has been located.
- **DEVELOPMENT RECOMMENDATION:** A software-control suggestion, not a legal conclusion.
- **CONFIGURABLE PENDING CONFIRMATION:** A product setting may represent an unresolved choice but is not approved as legally correct.
- **IMPLEMENTATION BLOCKED:** Do not treat the disputed production calculation or workflow as legally approved.
- **UNVERIFIED:** Available evidence does not establish a required fact or effective-date condition.

No payroll calculation, rate, threshold, migration or production behavior was changed for this documentation update.
