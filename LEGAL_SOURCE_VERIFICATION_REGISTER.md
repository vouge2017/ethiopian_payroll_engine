## Consistent unresolved matters

- **Transport/per diem:** Directive identity/currentness, exact Amharic formula, salary base, categories and substantiation remain unconfirmed; figures are provisional and implementation is blocked pending confirmation.
- **Filed PAYE correction:** Assessment limitation is distinct from monthly return correction. Current operational route, liability charges, credit/refund, portal, evidence and deadline are **OFFICIAL GUIDANCE UNVERIFIED**; workflow is blocked.
- **Pension:** Article 52(2) is confirmed as the multi-employer provision. The 45-day count/commencement/retroactivity and election operation require interpretation. Directive 1085/2025 effective date is **UNVERIFIED** because Article 74 conditions are not evidenced as satisfied.
- **Public holiday/overtime:** Stated statutory rates are supported; overlap/monthly salary/hour divisor calculation remains interpretation-required and blocked.
- **Annual leave:** Entitlement and statutory wage principles are supported; wage-component application/divisor remain interpretation-required; unconfirmed live calculation is blocked.

## Method and limits

Official Ministry of Justice and Ministry of Revenue sources were preferred. The Ministry of Revenue technical note is treated as explanatory material, not as binding law. Secondary sources were not used to override primary text. The official Justice amendment Gazette and pension proclamation/directive PDFs were inspected. The original Proc. 983 copy was non-government-hosted. No Ministry of Revenue monthly PAYE amendment guide, dated Article 74 registration evidence, Administration-site upload/publication evidence, adviser correspondence, or regulator response was obtained. No claim of legal approval or adviser acceptance is made.
