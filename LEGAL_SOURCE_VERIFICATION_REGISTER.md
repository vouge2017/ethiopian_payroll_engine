## Method and limits

Official Ministry of Justice materials were preferred. Secondary legal or payroll publications were used to identify conflicting claims, not to override primary text. ego-browser was attempted as requested, but the Justice site connection reset; direct public-source retrieval was used for verification. No private adviser or regulator correspondence was accessed. No current Ministry of Revenue PAYE-amendment guidance was identified. No adviser acceptance is claimed.
