# Phase 0 Evidence and External Validation Register

**As of:** 3 November 2026
**Purpose:** Evidence for product decisions only. This register is not legal advice, a provider agreement, a system integration, or proof that EthioPayroll can submit, pay or reconcile through an external service.

## Evidence classifications

- **Official primary source:** Enacted legal source, regulator/authority publication or provider’s own technical/product material.
- **Official draft:** Public document expressly marked draft; policy direction only, not binding requirement or operational service.
- **Secondary/partner source:** Public statement by a non-authority party or partner; may support a narrow factual claim but cannot establish the authority’s current procedure.
- **Professional interpretation required:** A source exists but its application requires qualified Ethiopian advice.
- **Unverified:** Direct source, current procedure, agreement or access evidence is missing or could not be inspected.

“Last checked” records the actual source inspection date where known. A failed fetch is recorded as an attempt, not a successful source verification. References last inspected on 2 November have not been silently represented as rechecked on 3 November.

## Evidence conclusion

- **Verified on 3 November:** MoJ’s record labels Proclamation 1321/2024 in force; NBE’s current strategy page labels the 2026–2030 document draft; Ethio telecom’s developer portal advertises a safe build/test environment.
- **Previously inspected but not refreshed on 3 November:** NBE payment-instrument register, ONPS/02/2020, Bank of Abyssinia corporate product page, POESSA materials and the Directive 1085 record dates. Preserve their 2 November check date until rechecked.
- **Unverified:** Current MoR/PAYE procedure and e-Tax portal operation; current Directive 1085 effective-date evidence; EthioPayroll provider access/contracts; a live salary-batch API; current provider fees/limits; current hosting/data region and lawful transfer analysis; selected attendance or accounting connector.

Do not represent a draft strategy as an operational API, a product page as an EthioPayroll integration, a generated file as submitted, a request as accepted, or a provider status as settled without the matching evidence.
