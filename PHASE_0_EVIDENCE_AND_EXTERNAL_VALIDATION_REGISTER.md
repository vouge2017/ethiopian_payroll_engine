# Phase 0 Evidence and External Validation Register

**As of:** 2 November 2026
**Purpose:** Record public evidence relevant to product direction, provider feasibility and privacy. This is not legal advice, a provider agreement or proof that EthioPayroll has an integration.

## Evidence classifications

- **Official primary evidence:** Government, regulator, authority or provider’s own law, directive, register, policy or product documentation.
- **Official draft:** Public document clearly marked draft; policy proposal only.
- **Secondary evidence:** Non-authoritative commentary or third-party report.
- **Professional interpretation required:** Source exists but its application needs qualified Ethiopian advice.
- **Assumption:** Product hypothesis without validation.
- **Unverified:** No usable public evidence or direct acceptance was established in this review.

## Current evidence conclusion

- **Verified publicly:** Ethiopian personal-data proclamation is in force; Telebirr is listed as a licensed commercialized payment instrument issuer; Telebirr bulk organizational payment is described publicly; a developer portal/testbed and merchant-payment APIs exist; NBE rules identify EATS-ACH as interbank clearing; Bank of Abyssinia describes corporate bulk payments/approval roles; MoR e-tax and Telebirr tax-payment channel are described in official sources; POESSA publishes its collection mandate and legal materials.
- **Not verified:** EthioPayroll access/contract to any provider; salary-batch Telebirr API for this product; live ACH/open corporate salary API; provider terms, fees and limits for the intended flow; official EthioPayroll tax/pension submission route or API; current PAYE correction process; exact pension directive effective date/registration evidence; hosting location or lawful cross-border processing for the current deployment.
- **Required before real employee data or financial execution:** written Ethiopian privacy/legal assessment, exact hosting/subprocessor evidence, customer consent/contract controls, provider/authority acceptance, and a qualified payroll professional’s written calculation review.
