## Evidence decisions cannot replace

- **Customer research:** buyer, first pilot profile, language needs, pricing, attendance sources, current accounting/payroll workflow, support expectations and willingness to change.
- **Ethiopian professional/authority review:** tax, pension and labour calculations; submission/correction workflow; privacy roles, hosting/transfer and retention; payment intermediation/authorization.
- **Provider/bank evidence:** contracts, product eligibility, sandbox, files/APIs, fees, limits, beneficiary checking, authorization, transaction status, settlement, reversal and support.
- **Technical evidence:** tenant isolation, role enforcement, calculation consistency, locked-history integrity, recovery, security, deployment location, backup/restore and exact-SHA test results.

## Management approval record template

For each decision record: Decision ID; selected option; rationale; approver role; date; evidence reviewed; conditions; reopening trigger. Leave blank until an authorized management decision is recorded. Do not use APPROVED status in the register until this record exists.

## Proposed approval sequence

1. M01: endorse, revise or reject the North Star and operating principle.
2. M02/M03: choose the first segment to validate and provisional business/accountant operating model.
3. M04/M05: lock bounded first-phase requirements and approval responsibility.
4. M06/M07/M08: approve pilot gates, legal/calculation governance and explicit real-data/transaction stop gates.
5. Obtain customer, professional, provider and technical evidence before M08 can authorize a real-data pilot.
6. Approve the baseline only after evidence is reflected; approve each roadmap release separately.
