## Status and priority definitions

- **OPEN:** question has no selected option.
- **RESEARCH REQUIRED:** external/customer evidence is needed before a decision can be made responsibly.
- **PROPOSED:** a recommendation is offered for management approval; it is not approved.
- **DEFERRED:** intentionally postponed with a reopening trigger.
- **APPROVED:** may be used only when a dated management record names the approver role, selected option, rationale and conditions. No D01–D25 decision is currently marked APPROVED.
- **P0:** resolve before approving the first Product Baseline; **P1:** before a real-business pilot; **P2:** before commercial release; **P3:** defer with a trigger.

## Decisions required for the first baseline versus deferred

**Baseline-blocking decisions:** D01–D04, D06–D16 and the D17 manual-handoff boundary. These must define the intended first user, bounded workflow, responsibility, permissions, approval, supported language, calculations, data handling, support, export, save/recovery and pilot gates. Some may be explicitly limited rather than fully resolved—for example, no direct payment or electronic filing integration. D12’s rule-specific professional approvals and D13’s privacy/hosting evidence are required before affected real-data use even if the baseline documents the unresolved state.

**Can remain deferred from the first baseline:** D05 final pricing (but commercial release needs it); direct integrations under D17; attendance capture/connectors D18; provider account verification D19; salary initiation D20; electronic submissions D21; system-specific connectors D22; voice D23; AI features under D24; public API/generalized integration platform under D25. D24 governance must exist before any AI functionality is released. D25 only permits internal contracts needed by an approved requirement.

## Management approval sequence

1. Endorse, revise or reject the proposed North Star without treating it as release scope.
2. Approve the customer hypothesis to validate, workspace/access proposal, first-release approval boundary and one/three/ten gate definitions.
3. Approve a bounded first-phase baseline using the template in `PROPOSED_PRODUCT_BASELINE_STRUCTURE.md`; mark all requirements and related D decisions with explicit approval status.
4. Obtain customer, professional, provider and technical evidence for the specific pilot profile. Do not use management approval to waive these gates.
5. Complete internal synthetic Gate 1; separately authorize one supervised parallel payroll only when its entry evidence is complete.
6. Review written accountant and customer evidence before each later go/no-go.
