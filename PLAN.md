### Payroll-rule comparison and source quality

Tests prove behavior against their authored expectations, not legal correctness. The repository audit did not find a complete primary-source-backed 2025 tax rule register. Do not silently change statutory calculations based on a code comment or secondary summary.

1. **PAYE bands / Proclamation 1395/2025:** `tax.py:4–23,39–54,87–128` contains marginal brackets 0% to ETB 2,000, then 15%, 20%, 25%, 30% and 35% above ETB 14,000; it can use active country/effective-date `TaxRule` records (`models.py:1587–1635`) and falls back to hardcoded defaults. The official Gazette text/effective date for 1395/2025 was not established in the original source run; its own header marks article/effective-date questions for confirmation. **Do not treat the bands/date as primary-source verified.**
2. **Pension deduction from taxable base:** `constants.PENSION_DEDUCTED_BEFORE_TAX` is true by default; `payroll.py:256–270` deducts employee pension before PAYE, and `settlement_service.py:173–178` does the same for outstanding salary. `tax.py:25–28` says this is practice pending accountant confirmation, but `models.py:1655–1658` labels `before_tax` as Ethiopian law. That internal contradiction conflicts with the requested gross-taxable-base rule and is a critical discrepancy, not a settled implementation choice.
3. **Pension:** `pension.py:4–16,24–30,118–159` defaults to 7% employee/11% employer on basic salary, with an optional ceiling. `employee_pension(basic_salary, for_date)` has no inputs for 45-day qualification, nationality or other-employer contributions. The official [Proclamation 1268/2022](https://justice.gov.et/en/law/private-organization-employees-pension-proclamation/) defines covered private employees as salaried employees employed at least 45 days and establishes the 7%/11% contributions; official [Directive 1085/2025](https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/1085-Private-Organizations-Employees-Pension-Implementation-Directive-No.-1085-2025.pdf) elaborates categories and multiple-employer rules. The law does not state whether the 45 days must be continuous/aggregated or whether contributions are retroactive to the first day or due if employment ends earlier; these cases remain unconfirmed. Employees with multiple private employers choose one employer’s salary for collection; a concurrent public-servant case is separately treated. Individual foreign-national and prior-pension cases require checking the statute/directive text, not a general eligibility shortcut.
4. **Work-travel and commuting transport:** `services/allowance_service.py:15–18,56–70,102–108` hard-codes the lower of ETB 2,200 or 25% of basic for a `transport` allowance and does not cap exemption by contractual allowance. No separate home-to-work ETB 600 rule was found in this path. Recent [Sage 2025/2026 payroll summaries](https://za-kb.sage.com/portal/app/portlets/results/viewdocument.jsp?solutionid=260116041430250) attribute those limits and the contractual cap to Directive 1/2011; however, the authenticated directive and its current legal force were not located. Treat the values as secondary guidance until the primary text is obtained.
5. **Per diem:** `allowance_service.py:117–123` computes `max(ETB 255 × 30, 4% of basic)` and then caps at ETB 2,200; it has no trip-distance input, day count or manager/staff category. The latest Sage guide reports a **lower-of** daily formula for travel beyond 25 km: normal staff lower of 4% of monthly basic or ETB 500/day; managers/assistant managers lower of 5% or ETB 1,000/day. An older [GPA guide](https://gpa.net/blogs/latest-news/ethiopia-s-payroll-regulations-unveiled) instead says higher-of and ETB 255/day. Because the underlying Directive 1/2011 was not available from an official source, neither wording can be certified as the binding rule; the current code’s formula does not implement the Sage-reported daily/category rule.
6. **Severance, termination salary and leave cash-out:** `settlement_service.py:159–203` adds severance and leave encashment to `total_earnings` but calculates `tax_on_salary` only on `outstanding_salary` (less pension if the flag is on). Against the supplied rule that these components are taxable, no withholding for severance/leave encashment was found in this settlement function. `severance.py:4–31,147–239` implements the separate labour-law entitlement; taxability and entitlement must not be conflated.
7. **Casual unskilled worker:** no explicit exemption/aggregation path was found in the payroll calculator for the stated same-employer, 30 days or less within 12 months case. Confirm the current Income Tax Regulation and conditions before implementing; no unsupported exemption should be inferred from tenure alone.
8. **Holiday and overtime:** `overtime.py:4–21,29–44` uses `/208`, 1.5× day, 1.75× night, 2× `holiday` and 2.5× `rest_day_holiday`. The labels/comments conflict: `holiday` is 2× and is labelled Public Holiday, while the comment describes the 2× multiplier as weekly rest-day work; the 2.5× key is labelled “Rest Day + Holiday”. Under [Labour Proclamation 1156/2019](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eth204430.pdf), Art. 75(1) is ordinary public-holiday work at 2× hourly wage; Art. 68(1)(d) is overtime on a public holiday at 2.5× ordinary hourly rate plus normal wage; Art. 68(1)(c) is overtime on the weekly rest day at 2×. These are distinct cases, so the current entry labels make the rate selection ambiguous and can underpay holiday overtime. Daily cap is checked per entry and weekly cap over the whole submitted list (`overtime.py:205–280`), only as a warning; the calculator has no date-aware grouping to enforce four hours/day and 12/week. The 208-hour divisor is not established by the primary labour text reviewed. No official interpretation resolving holiday/night/rest-day overlap was found; do not assume stacking.
9. **Leave pay/divisor:** `services/settlement_service.py:79–126` calculates unused leave at `(basic_salary + allowances) / 30` per day; when a balance row is missing it assumes no leave was taken. Under Labour Proclamation 1156/2019, Arts. 53 and 77, leave pay is what the worker would have received if work continued, listed categories such as overtime, per diem, specified travel/transport or relocation allowances, bonuses and commissions are excluded from “wages,” and unused leave is paid at termination. Art. 77(5) provides proportional leave for under one year; Art. 77(3)’s 26 days equals one month only for counting service, not as a daily-pay divisor. No annual-leave-specific averaging period or divisor was found in the law reviewed. The code’s flat 30-day divisor and undifferentiated allowance total therefore need legal/accountant review; do not borrow the separate severance divisor as leave law.
10. **PAYE filed-return corrections:** Tax Administration Amendment Proclamation 1434/2026 (official [Ministry of Justice law page and Gazette text](https://justice.gov.et/en/law/tax-administration-proclamation-amendment-proclamation-no-1434-2026/)) inserted assessment-revision rules at Arts. 96–102. They specify grounds, materiality/evidence and notice requirements, and allow taxpayer-initiated or authority-initiated review. The taxpayer restriction found is to apply before the issue is reviewed on appeal; the five-year/one-year period in Art. 99(2) is the Authority’s limit, not a published taxpayer filing deadline. No public eTax step-by-step correction workflow, PAYE-specific amended form, attachment list or extra-tax payment deadline was located. Earlier Tax Administration Proclamation 983/2016 Arts. 49–50 describe credits/refunds, including set-off and written refund requests, but post-1434/2026 renumbering/current text must be confirmed before relying on the old article numbers or timing. The public [eTax page](https://etax.mor.gov.et/) only exposes a gated login/register landing page. No portal account was accessed and no submission was made.

The leave/hours conclusions above use the Federal Negarit Gazette text of Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019 hosted by FAOLEX, corroborated by a secondary legal transcription; it is not a consolidated official English amendment register. For pension, the [Ministry of Justice Proclamation 1268/2022 record](https://justice.gov.et/en/law/private-organization-employees-pension-proclamation/), official Gazette PDF and Directive 1085/2025 were opened. For filed-return corrections, official Ministry of Justice pages and Gazette text for Proclamations 983/2016 and 1434/2026 were reviewed; no eTax manual/form was publicly readable. For transport/per diem, the accessible numeric rule was from commercial Sage guidance, while an older GPA article conflicts; the authentic Directive 1/2011 and its amendment/repeal history remain open source gates. The [Ministry of Finance Income Tax Regulation 410/2017](https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/c0/31/c0319984-501a-4eab-bf5b-98c15fa7985c/income_tax_regulation_english_clean.pdf), [Proclamation 979/2016 record](https://justice.gov.et/en/law/federal-income-tax-proclamation/) and [Tax Compliance Guide](https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/86/fb/86fb37d6-8405-45ad-b439-560b3e818d45/tax_compliance_guide_for_foreign_investors_in_ethiopia.pdf) do not by themselves resolve the missing directive text or complete the 1395/2025 PAYE verification.
