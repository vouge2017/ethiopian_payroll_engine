## Notes for Translators

### Amharic (አማርኛ)
- Use standard Amharic business/HR terminology
- Use Ethiopic script (Ge'ez script)
- Keep technical terms like "ERCA", "PSSSA", "TIN", "PDF" in English
- "Payroll" → "የደመወዝ አሰላሰፍ" or keep as "Payroll"
- "Pension" → "የጡረታ ገንዘብ" or "የጡረታ አበቦ"

### Afaan Oromoo
- Use Qubee (Latin script)
- Use standard Oromoo business terminology
- Keep technical terms like "ERCA", "PSSSA", "TIN", "PDF" in English
- "Payroll" → "Kaffaltii" (salary/wage)
- "Employee" → "Hojjetaa" (worker)
- "Tax" → "Ramaddii"
- "Pension" → "Furtuu hojjetaa"
