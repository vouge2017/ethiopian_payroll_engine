# How to calculate income tax in Ethiopia (2025 brackets explained)

Ethiopia uses a progressive tax system with six brackets. For the 2025 fiscal year (effective Hamle 1, 2017 EFY), the brackets are:

| Taxable Income (ETB) | Rate |
|----------------------|------|
| 0 – 2,000            | 0%   |
| 2,001 – 4,000        | 15%  |
| 4,001 – 7,000        | 20%  |
| 7,001 – 10,000       | 25%  |
| 10,001 – 14,000      | 30%  |
| Above 14,000         | 35%  |

## How it works

Tax is calculated **marginally**: each portion of income within a bracket is taxed at that bracket’s rate. For example, an employee earning ETB 9,000 gross:

- First 2,000 ETB: 0 % → 0 ETB
- Next 2,000 ETB (2,001‑4,000): 15 % → 300 ETB
- Next 3,000 ETB (4,001‑7,000): 20 % → 600 ETB
- Remaining 2,000 ETB (7,001‑9,000): 25 % → 500 ETB
**Total tax = 1,400 ETB**

## Why this matters for payroll

- Employers must withhold this amount each month and remit it to the Ethiopian Revenues and Customs Authority (ERCA).
- Under‑ or over‑withholding can lead to penalties or cash‑flow issues.
