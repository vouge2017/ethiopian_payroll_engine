How It Works

Each payroll run generates a balanced journal entry:

Debits
  • 5100 — Salary Expense (gross)
  • 5200 — Employer Pension Expense
Credits
  • 2100 — PAYE Tax Payable
  • 2200 — Pension Payable (Employee)
  • 2210 — Pension Payable (Employer)
  • 1000 — Bank/Cash (net pay)

Account numbers are examples. Adjust them to match your chart of accounts.
