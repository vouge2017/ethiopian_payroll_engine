How Tax Works

Tax Brackets (Monthly)
ETB 0 – 2,0000%
ETB 2,001 – 4,00015%
ETB 4,001 – 7,00020%
ETB 7,001 – 10,00025%
ETB 10,001 – 14,00030%
ETB 14,001+35%
How Your Payroll Is Calculated
  1. Gross = Basic Salary + Allowances + Overtime
  2. Pension = 7% of Basic Salary (deducted before tax)
  3. Taxable = Gross − Pension − Exempt Allowances
  4. Tax = Progressive brackets on Taxable − ETB 150 relief
  5. Net Pay = Gross − Pension − Tax − Other Deductions

Pension is deducted before tax — this is a legal requirement that reduces your tax burden.
