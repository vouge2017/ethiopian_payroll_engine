What we need from your spreadsheet

  • Employee name Any column named "name", "employee", "staff" — auto-detected
  • Gross salary Total before deductions. If you only have basic salary, that works too
  • Tax (PAYE) If you report it — we compare and check against Proclamation 1395/2025
  • Employee pension If you report it — must be 7% of basic (Proclamation 1268/2022)
  • Net pay Take-home after all deductions
  • Basic salary If separate from gross — needed for precise pension/tax brackets
  • Allowances, overtime, employee ID, phone, TIN More columns = more precise comparison

What we compare & the law behind it

FieldRule
Gross salaryBasic + allowances + overtime. Your sheet may combine what we separate.
Tax (PAYE)Proclamation 1395/2025, Article 11. Pension (7%) is deducted before taxable income.
Employee pensionProclamation 1268/2022, Article 12: 7% of basic salary, no ceiling.
Net payGross − pension − tax. We trace which component caused the gap.
Example: If your sheet shows net ETB 8,420 and our engine calculates ETB 8,145:
  • Tax: Yours 2,580 vs Ours 2,955 — engine deducts pension first, saving ETB 105 tax
  • Pension: Yours 0 vs Ours 700 — 7% of basic required by Proclamation 1268/2022
  • Net diff: ETB 275 — caused by missing pension deduction
