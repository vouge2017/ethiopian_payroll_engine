About eTax Filing

ERCA monthly tax filing is done through the eTax portal. The process:

  1. Generate the ERCA export from EthioPayroll (Reports → ERCA Export)
  2. Log into the eTax portal for your regional/sub-city office
  3. Upload or enter the data using the eTax template
  4. Save the confirmation number in EthioPayroll (Reports → Mark as Filed)

Note: Column format may vary by regional office. Configure your ERCA export columns in Settings → Report Templates to match your eTax template.
