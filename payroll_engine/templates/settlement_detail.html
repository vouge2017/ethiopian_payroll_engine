Legal Reference

Severance calculation per Labor Proclamation No. 1156/2019, Articles 40-42:

  • Severance = 30 days' wages for year 1 + 1/3 of that per further year
  • Maximum cap: 12 months of salary
  • Applies to: redundancy, mutual agreement
  • Does not apply to: resignation, termination for cause
  • Severance, notice pay and leave encashment are employment income (Income Tax Proc. 979/2016 Art. 12(1)(c); rates per Proc. 1395/2025)
