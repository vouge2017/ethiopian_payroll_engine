BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few useful answers.

Clear expectations from the first click.

What do I need to get started?

An Ethiopian phone number and a password. Registration accepts 9 digits starting with 7 or 9, with +251 shown separately. You’ll add your name and company in the next step.

Does approval pay my employees?

No. Approval preserves the reviewed payroll amounts. Payment is a separate step; downloading a bank file does not itself transfer money.

Can I use it on my phone?

The workspace includes responsive phone layouts for preparation, review and approval. Detailed employee information opens when you need it.

Where do employees find their payslips?

Employees with an existing account can sign in to their employee workspace to view available payslips. If your company has given you an account, use Sign in rather than creating another company.

Is the payroll example a live calculation?

No. The example shows synthetic saved amounts to explain the review layout. Your actual payroll is calculated from your company’s saved employee details and monthly inputs.
