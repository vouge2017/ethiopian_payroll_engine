# Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019 — Key Findings

**Full title:** Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019
**Published:** Federal Negarit Gazette No. 89, 5th September, 2019
**Pages:** 112 (bilingual Amharic/English)

---

## ⚠️ CRITICAL DISCREPANCIES FOUND

### 1. Overtime Rates (Article 68) — WRONG IN SYSTEM

| Type | Law Says | Our System | Status |
|------|----------|-----------|--------|
| Day (6am-10pm) | **1.5×** | 1.25× | ❌ WRONG |
| Night (10pm-6am) | **1.75×** | 1.50× | ❌ WRONG |
| Weekly rest day | **2.0×** | 2.0× | ✅ Correct |
| Public holiday | **2.5×** | 2.5× | ✅ Correct |

**Impact:** Every overtime payment is underpaid by 20-25%.

### 2. Overtime Limits (Article 67(2)) — WRONG IN SYSTEM

| Limit | Law Says | Our System | Status |
|-------|----------|-----------|--------|
| Daily max | **4 hours/day** | Not tracked | ❌ Missing |
| Weekly max | **12 hours/week** | Not tracked | ❌ Missing |
| Monthly max | Not in law | 20 hours/month | ⚠️ Not in law |
| Yearly max | Not in law | 100 hours/year | ⚠️ Not in law |

**Note:** The law limits overtime to 4 hours/day and 12 hours/week. There is NO monthly or yearly cap in the law. Our system's 20/month and 100/year limits are not from this proclamation.

### 3. Annual Leave (Article 77) — WRONG IN SYSTEM

| Rule | Law Says | Our System | Status |
|------|----------|-----------|--------|
| Year 1 | **16 days** | 14 days | ❌ WRONG |
| Increment | **+1 day per 2 years** | +1 day per year | ❌ WRONG |
| Max | Not specified | 30 days | ⚠️ Not in law |

### 4. Severance Pay (Article 40) — FORMULA WRONG

The law says:
- Year 1: **30 days** of average daily wages
- Each additional year: **+1/3 of the base** (≈10 days per year)
- Cap: **12 months** wages

**Our system:** `salary × years` — This is too simple and overpays for longer tenures.

**Correct formula:**
- 1 year: 30 days
- 2 years: 30 + 10 = 40 days
- 3 years: 30 + 20 = 50 days
- ...
- Cap: 365 days (12 months)

### 5. Special Leave (Article 81(3)) — WRONG IN SYSTEM

| Rule | Law Says | Our System | Status |
|------|----------|-----------|--------|
| Special leave | **5 days (unpaid)**, max 2×/year | 3 days | ❌ WRONG |
