## Sections Relevant to Payroll Engine

### 1. Employment Income Tax Brackets (Art. 11) — ✅ VERIFIED
Our brackets match exactly. See `01_TAX_BRACKETS.md`.

### 2. Cash Payment Limit (Art. 81) — ❌ NEEDS UPDATE
Changed from ETB 30,000 to ETB 50,000. See `02_CASH_PAYMENT_LIMIT.md`.

### 3. Personal Relief — ⚠️ NOT FOUND IN THIS PROCLAMATION
The new Article 11 has no personal relief. Must check original 979/2016.

### 4. Pension — ⚠️ NOT IN THIS PROCLAMATION
Pension is governed by Proclamation No. 1268/2022 (separate law).

### 5. Overtime, Leave, Severance — ⚠️ NOT IN THIS PROCLAMATION
These are governed by Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019 (separate law).

---

## What This Proclamation Does NOT Change

- ❌ Personal relief (not mentioned)
- ❌ Pension rates (separate law)
- ❌ Overtime rates (separate law)
- ❌ Leave entitlements (separate law)
- ❌ Severance formula (separate law)
- ❌ ERCA filing format (administrative, not in this law)

---

## Key Takeaway

This proclamation **only confirms our tax brackets are correct** and **updates the cash payment limit from 30K to 50K**. All other payroll rules (pension, overtime, leave, severance, personal relief) come from other proclamations.
