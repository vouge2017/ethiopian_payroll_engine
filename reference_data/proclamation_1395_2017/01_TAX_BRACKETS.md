# Employment Income Tax Brackets — Article 11

**Source:** Proclamation No. 1395/2025, Article 6 (amending Article 11 of Proclamation 979/2016)
**Effective:** 1 September 2025 (Hamle 26, 2017 E.C.)

## The Law (verbatim from Gazette)

> "11. Employment Income Tax Rates
>
> The monthly rate of employment income tax is:"

| Monthly Income from Employment (in Birr) | Employment Income Tax Rate |
|------------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| 0 – 2,000                               | 0%                        |
| 2,001 – 4,000                           | 15%                       |
| 4,001 – 7,000                           | 20%                       |
| 7,001 – 10,000                          | 25%                       |
| 10,001 – 14,000                         | 30%                       |
| Over 14,000                             | 35%                       |

## ⚠️ CRITICAL FINDING: Personal Relief

**The new Article 11 (as replaced by Proclamation 1395/2025) contains ONLY the tax bracket table. There is NO personal relief provision in Article 11.**

The previous Article 11 (from 979/2016) may have included a personal relief of ETB 150/month, but the 2025 amendment replaced the entire article with just the bracket table.

**Implications:**
- If personal relief was only in old Article 11 → it was **repealed** by this amendment
- If personal relief was in a different article of 979/2016 → it may still be in effect
- The original 979/2016 proclamation must be checked to determine if personal relief exists elsewhere

**Action required:** Obtain and verify the original Proclamation 979/2016 to confirm whether personal relief is defined in a different article.

## Also Amended: Article 12

The amendment adds Sub-Articles (5), (6), and (7) after Article 12(4) of the original proclamation. These deal with:
- Diplomatic staff registration (Art. 12(5)-(6))
- Work permit notification to Tax Authority (Art. 12(7))

Article 12 itself (withholding tax from employment) was NOT amended — only new sub-articles were added. The withholding mechanism and any personal relief in Article 12 remain as they were in 979/2016.
