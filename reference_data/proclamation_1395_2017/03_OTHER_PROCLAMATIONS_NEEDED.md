# Other Proclamations Needed for Full Verification

Proclamation 1395/2025 only amends the Income Tax Act. To fully verify all 34 statutory rules in the Verification Package, we also need:

## Required (not yet obtained)

### 1. Proclamation No. 979/2016 — Federal Income Tax Proclamation (Original)
**Why needed:** This is the BASE law that 1395/2025 amends. The personal relief provision (ETB 150/month) is likely defined here, possibly in:
- Article 11 (old version, before amendment)
- Article 12 (withholding tax from employment)
- Or another article on employment income deductions

**Critical question:** Does the old Article 11 or Article 12 of 979/2016 contain a personal relief provision?

### 2. Proclamation No. 1268/2022 — Private Organizations Employees Pension
**Why needed:** Covers:
- Employee pension rate (currently 7% in our system)
- Employer pension rate (currently 11% in our system)
- Pension salary ceiling (currently "none" in our system)
- Basic salary vs gross salary for pension calculation

### 3. Proclamation No. 1156/2019 — Labour Proclamation
**Why needed:** Covers:
- Overtime rates (day 1.25×, night 1.5×, holiday 2×, rest+holiday 2.5×)
- Overtime limits (20 hrs/month, 100 hrs/year)
- Annual leave (14 days year 1, +1/day per year, max 30)
- Sick leave (180 days, tiers at 100%/50%/0%)
- Maternity leave (120 days)
- Paternity leave (3 days)
- Severance formula and cap (12 months)

## Priority Order

1. **979/2016** — Resolves the personal relief question (highest impact, affects every payslip)
2. **1268/2022** — Verifies pension rates (affects every payslip)
3. **1156/2019** — Verifies overtime/leave/severance (affects specific scenarios)
