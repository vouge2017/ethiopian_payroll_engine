# Original Tax Brackets — Proclamation 979/2016, Article 11

**Source:** Federal Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016, Article 11
**Status:** SUPERSEDED by Proclamation 1395/2025, Article 11 (new brackets effective Sept 2025)

## Old Article 11 — Employment Income Tax Rates (REPEALED)

| Employment Income (per month) Birr | Tax Rate |
|-----------------------------------|----------|
| 0 – 600                           | 0%       |
| 601 – 1,650                       | 10%      |
| 1,651 – 3,200                     | 15%      |
| 3,201 – 5,250                     | 20%      |
| 5,251 – 7,800                     | 25%      |
| 7,801 – 10,900                    | 30%      |
| Over 10,900                       | 35%      |

## ⚠️ FINDING: No Personal Relief in Current Law

Article 10(3) explicitly states:

> "An employee shall not be allowed a deduction for any expenditure incurred in deriving employment income."

**No personal relief exists under Proclamation 979/2016 or its 2025 amendment.**

If an ETB 150 relief ever existed, it would have been under an earlier proclamation or directive predating 979/2016. We have no evidence of such a provision in current law.

## New Article 11 (from Proclamation 1395/2025)

The 2025 amendment replaced Article 11 entirely with new brackets:

| Monthly Income from Employment (in Birr) | Tax Rate |
|------------------------------------------|----------|
| 0 – 2,000                               | 0%       |
| 2,001 – 4,000                           | 15%      |
| 4,001 – 7,000                           | 20%      |
| 7,001 – 10,000                          | 25%      |
| 10,001 – 14,000                         | 30%      |
| Over 14,000                             | 35%      |

## What Changed

| Bracket | Old (979/2016) | New (1395/2025) |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| 0%      | 0–600         | 0–2,000         |
| 10%     | 601–1,650     | (removed)       |
| 15%     | 1,651–3,200   | 2,001–4,000     |
| 20%     | 3,201–5,250   | 4,001–7,000     |
| 25%     | 5,251–7,800   | 7,001–10,000    |
| 30%     | 7,801–10,900  | 10,001–14,000   |
| 35%     | Over 10,900   | Over 14,000     |

Key changes:
- 10% bracket eliminated
- All thresholds raised significantly (tax cut for most earners)
- 0% bracket expanded from 600 to 2,000
