Upload your payroll spreadsheet

We accept .xlsx, .xls, or .csv files. Your file is read-only — we never save it or change your data.

What happens

  1. We read your spreadsheet and detect the columns automatically.
  2. For each row, we match it to an employee in your system (by name, phone, or TIN).
  3. We run the EthioPayroll engine on your inputs (basic salary, allowances, overtime).
  4. We display a side-by-side comparison: your numbers vs ours, with the reason for every difference.
  5. You can download the full comparison as an Excel file to share with your accountant.

What we compare

FieldWhere the difference usually comes from
Gross salary Your sheet may include overtime, bonuses, or other additions that our engine calculates separately. We use your basic + allowances as inputs — if your gross is higher, check if overtime is baked in.
Tax (PAYE) The most common difference. Our engine deducts 7% employee pension from gross before calculating tax (Proclamation 1395/2025, Article 11 + Proclamation 1268/2022, Article 12). If your sheet does not deduct pension first, taxable income is higher and you are over-taxed.
Employee pension Fixed at 7% of basic salary (Proclamation 1268/2022, Article 12). If your sheet applies pension to gross instead of basic, or applies a ceiling that does not exist in law, the number will differ.
Net pay The result of gross − pension − tax. If your net differs, the cause is in one of the above fields. We show the exact cause for each row.

Example: what a difference looks like

If your spreadsheet shows a net pay of ETB 8,420.00 for Dawit and our engine calculates ETB 8,145.00, the diff check will show:

Row 3 — Dawit Mekonnen (matched)
Gross salary: Yours ETB 15,000.00 vs Ours ETB 15,000.00 (match)
Tax (PAYE): Yours ETB 2,580.00 vs Ours ETB 2,955.00 (difference: -ETB 375.00)
Our engine deducts pension (ETB 700.00, 7% of basic) before calculating tax, saving ETB 105.00 in tax. Check if your sheet deducts pension before tax.
Employee pension (7%): Yours ETB 0.00 vs Ours ETB 700.00 (difference: -ETB 700.00)
Your sheet shows zero pension. Proclamation 1268/2022, Article 12 requires 7% of basic salary with no ceiling. Pension should be deducted.
Net pay: Yours ETB 8,420.00 vs Ours ETB 8,145.00 (difference: -ETB 275.00)
Your net is higher because you missed the pension deduction.
